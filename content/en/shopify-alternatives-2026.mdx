# Alternatives to Shopify (2026): quick comparison and how to choose

The real decision is not “Shopify or not Shopify”—it’s **which platform you can operate** with your margin, team skills, and growth goals. This page helps you frame the trade-offs without marketing hype.

<h2 id="when">1) When to consider an alternative to Shopify</h2>

Consider switching if:

- you need deep technical control (highly customized architecture),
- recurring costs (apps, fees) hurt your margin at your current volume,
- your team is already optimized for WordPress/CRM/ERP and wants tighter in‑house integration,
- your priority is editorial content with a relatively simple store.

Shopify remains a strong default when you want a **high‑converting checkout**, solid security, and mature app ecosystem.

<h2 id="scenarios">3) Pick by scenario (decision shortcuts)</h2>

- **You want to launch fast and convert well:** Shopify is often the best starting point.
- **Your engine is content and WordPress SEO:** WooCommerce can be excellent if you keep performance under control.
- **You want the simplest operation possible:** Wix/Squarespace can be enough (watch scaling limits).
- **You have enterprise constraints:** compare Shopify Plus vs more customized stacks based on governance and integration needs.

Best criterion: your ability to **operate** the platform daily (time, skills, budget), not only a feature checklist.

<h2 id="migration">4) If you migrate (SEO, tracking, data)</h2>

Before moving, secure:

- URL structure and 301 redirects,
- analytics tracking (GA4, pixels, consent),
- product/collection mapping and data (SKU, pricing, images),
- SEO essentials: titles, meta, canonicals, schema, internal linking,
- performance (Core Web Vitals) after the move.

If you’re undecided, start with Shopify vs WooCommerce and a 12‑month TCO estimate.
