# Methodology

This page explains how EasyShopBuilder creates guides, checklists, and comparisons about Shopify.

## 1) What we evaluate

We prioritize levers with real impact:

- **Shopify SEO:** collection/product structure, internal links, indexation, content, schemas.
- **Performance:** Core Web Vitals, theme, images, scripts, apps.
- **Conversion (CRO):** product pages, collections, checkout, trust, offers.
- **Stack choices:** app selection, alternatives, common pitfalls.

## 2) How we make a recommendation

We try to:

- separate **universal** best practices from context-dependent advice (catalog size, country, budget),
- provide actionable checklists (step order),
- highlight trade-offs (e.g., speed vs features).

## 3) Sources & verification

We cross-check claims with official documentation, product pages (themes/apps), and well-known technical references. See: [Sources](/en/sources).

## 4) Affiliate transparency

The site may earn commissions from some affiliate links. We aim to keep guidance **useful and transparent**.

## 5) Updates

We update content when Shopify changes materially (features, pricing, SEO constraints, etc.).
