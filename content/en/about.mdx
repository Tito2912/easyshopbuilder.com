# About

EasyShopBuilder is an editorial site focused on Shopify: **SEO**, **conversion**, **themes**, **apps**, and ecommerce best practices.

## Our goal

Help you launch a clean, fast store (UX, performance, tracking), then grow qualified traffic and sales with structured SEO and CRO.

## Editorial independence & affiliate links

Some pages contain **affiliate links** (for example to Shopify). If you sign up through these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This supports publishing and keeping guides updated.

Affiliate relationships do not mean “guaranteed positive coverage”. We aim to stay practical: what works, what doesn’t, and in which contexts.

## Updates & corrections

Shopify changes fast (features, apps, pricing). We update key pages when meaningful changes happen. If you spot an issue, contact us with the URL and the correction.
