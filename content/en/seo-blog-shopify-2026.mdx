# Shopify blog SEO (2026)

The blog isn’t the goal—it’s a **lever**: capture informational searches (guides, comparisons) and **route** readers to money pages (collections/products) with a logical internal link structure.

<h2 id="role">1) Blog role in a Shopify store</h2>

Use the blog to:

- rank for “how to choose / comparison / maintenance” queries,
- build trust (E‑E‑A‑T) with sourced guides,
- strengthen internal linking to collections/products,
- reduce objections (FAQ, criteria, proof).

<h2 id="topics">2) Pick topics that convert</h2>

High‑ROI formats:

- “Best X for Y” buying guides,
- “X vs Y” comparisons,
- “How to…” tutorials,
- “Mistakes to avoid” / checklists.

Pick topics close to your offer: the more natural the link to a collection, the higher the ROI.

<h2 id="onpage">3) On-page structure (simple)</h2>

Article checklist:

- clear H1 + short intro + table of contents (H2/H3),
- executive summary up top (3 bullets),
- scannable sections + concrete examples,
- short FAQ when relevant,
- visible updated date.

Add Article schema and (when visible) FAQ schema.

<h2 id="links">4) Linking: blog → collections → products</h2>

Each article should link to:

- 1 primary collection,
- 2–5 products (if relevant) or sub-collections,
- 1–2 related articles (satellites) to reinforce the cluster.

Avoid forced links. Put them in helpful blocks (“recommended picks”, “see the collection”).

<h2 id="checklist">5) Quick checklist</h2>

- topic close to your offer
- strong H2/H3 structure + summary
- links to money pages + lateral links to satellites
- performance OK (images, scripts)
- Article/FAQ schema validated when used
