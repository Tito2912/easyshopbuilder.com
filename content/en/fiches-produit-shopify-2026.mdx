# Shopify product pages (2026): conversion structure

Your product page should do one thing: move a visitor from “I’m browsing” to “I’m deciding”. Conversion is often blocked by four objections: “is it for me?”, “is it trustworthy?”, “what’s the real total cost?”, “what if it doesn’t work?”.

<h2 id="structure">1) Structure (recommended order)</h2>

- clean hero: image + title + price + CTA,
- 3 benefits (real outcomes),
- proof (reviews, UGC, guarantees),
- shipping/returns info,
- specs table if needed,
- short FAQ.

<h2 id="trust">2) Trust & proof</h2>

High-impact reassurance:

- customer reviews (photos help),
- simple returns policy,
- realistic delivery estimates,
- warranty and support visibility.

Avoid fake badges. Two strong proofs beat twelve logos.

<h2 id="mobile">3) Mobile UX (where it’s decided)</h2>

- CTA visible without endless scrolling,
- variants (size/color) without errors,
- scannable sections (accordions OK, but don’t hide everything),
- performance: optimized images, minimal scripts.

<h2 id="faq">4) FAQ: kill objections</h2>

Examples:

- “Which size should I pick?”
- “Delivery and returns?”
- “Compatibility / care?”

Keep 3–6 questions that matter.

<h2 id="checklist">5) Checklist</h2>

- clear hero + CTA
- benefits + proof
- shipping/returns visible
- low-friction variants
- readable mobile UX
- one test hypothesis at a time (A/B)
