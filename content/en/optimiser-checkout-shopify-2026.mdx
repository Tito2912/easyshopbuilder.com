# Optimize Shopify checkout (2026)

Before “hacks”, lock fundamentals: **clear total cost**, **realistic delivery**, **simple returns**, **trust**. Then optimize (Shop Pay, upsells, recovery).

<h2 id="basics">1) Basics (highest impact)</h2>

- total cost visible early (shipping/taxes),
- clear delivery estimates,
- returns policy easy to find,
- helpful, readable error messages,
- mobile speed (no unnecessary scripts).

<h2 id="friction">2) Reduce friction (especially mobile)</h2>

Typical friction:

- too many fields,
- forms that reset,
- blocking popups,
- laggy clicks.

Action: test checkout on 3 devices on mobile data and list each micro-friction.

<h2 id="payments">3) Payments & Shop Pay</h2>

Express checkout helps, but:

- users still need trust,
- costs must stay transparent,
- flow must work when express fails.

Offer the methods your market expects, then measure impact on conversion and support.

<h2 id="shipping">4) Shipping & returns: the real anti-abandon lever</h2>

Abandonment often spikes when people discover:

- unexpected fees,
- long delivery times,
- unclear returns policy.

Surface key info on product pages and in cart: shipping cost range, delivery estimates, returns terms.

<h2 id="checklist">5) Checklist</h2>

- clear costs before payment
- solid mobile speed
- low-friction forms
- market-appropriate payment methods
- shipping/returns reassurance
- recovery flows + clean tracking
